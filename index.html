What is Kali Linux?

Kali Linux is a Debian-based operating system purpose-built for penetration testing, digital forensics, and security research. Instead of installing tools one at a time, Kali ships with hundreds of them — scanners, password crackers, wireless attack suites, exploitation frameworks, and forensic utilities — pre-configured and ready to run.

It's maintained by Offensive Security, the same organization behind the OSCP certification, and it's the operating system most security courses, capture-the-flag competitions, and professional pentest teams standardize on. This hub is an independent, community-style curriculum built around it — not an official Offensive Security product.

  • Free and open source, used by professionals, students, and CTF players alike
